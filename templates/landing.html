Scheduling Algorithms & Dispatch Heuristics

01 SEQUENTIAL

Round Robin (RR)

Sequentially assigns tasks across available workers in a fixed cyclic order. Each worker receives tasks in turn, ensuring fair distribution of workload across all active workers regardless of their current state.

  • Fair cyclic distribution across workers
  • No workload state consideration
  • Predictable task assignment pattern

02 WORKLOAD-AWARE

Least Connections (LC)

Selects the available worker with the lowest current workload at each scheduling decision. Workload is calculated as (executing task count) + (queued task count), minimizing average waiting time.

  • Minimizes average waiting time
  • Dynamic workload-based selection
  • Adapts to uneven task durations
